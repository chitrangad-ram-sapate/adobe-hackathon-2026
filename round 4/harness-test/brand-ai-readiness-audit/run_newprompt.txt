## Evidence for the top two findings

**F-001 · RND-006: Facts locked inside images** (high severity, high confidence, found by reading the page source)
> 232 images whose filename or position indicates they carry words (charts, price lists, banners, heading graphics) have no alt text, e.g. `https://static.airtable.com/images/marketing-nav-platform-graphic.webp` on `https://www.airtable.com/`.

- 232 of 568 sampled images are affected, across pages like `/`, `/about`, `/ai`, `/pricing`, `/templates`, `/developers` and `/articles`.
- Other examples: `marketing-nav-solution.webp`, `marketing-nav-resource.webp`, and several `homepage-variant-b/avatar-*.png` images.
- **Weak spot:** many of the examples are headshots (`avatar-sooyoung.png`, `avatar-gemma.png`) and menu graphics, not charts or price lists. The missing alt text is real, but "facts locked in images" probably overstates it. This overlaps with F-006 (most images have no alt text).

**F-003 · ANS-006: Pricing pages contain no readable price** (high severity, high confidence, found by reading the page source)
> 3 of 5 sampled pricing/product pages contain no numeric price in their text, e.g. `https://www.airtable.com/articles/product`.

- The three flagged pages are `/articles/product`, `/solutions/product` and `/lp/product/feature-prioritization`. None of them contains wording about getting a quote.
- **Likely a false positive:** these look like an article index, a solutions page and a landing page. They were probably classed as "pricing/product" pages only because "product" is in the URL. The actual `/pricing` page isn't among them, so check this before acting on it.

F-002 (the iframe finding) looks similarly doubtful. All five flagged pages have exactly 22 words and embed the same Marketo form widget. That suggests the pages are built in the browser (the same issue as F-005) rather than content sitting inside an iframe.

I left the report exactly as the pipeline wrote it and didn't change any severities. The audit reported no missed steps or gaps in coverage.
